PHIM NGƯỜI QUE LỊCH SỬ – HÀM TỬ – CHƯƠNG DƯƠNG 1285 (Trần Quang Khải · quân Nguyên Mông)
Phong cách A5 Historical Cartoon – quy trình skill directing-stickman-videos (kaomei/stickman-video-director, MIT).
Dán từng PROMPT CLIP vào Veo / Google Flow (chế độ text-to-video, 16:9, 8 giây). Nên tạo trước ảnh tham chiếu nhân vật (chính diện / nghiêng / sau lưng) và đính kèm mỗi lần tạo để giữ nhân vật nhất quán.

═══════════ PHASE A – ĐỀ XUẤT ĐẠO DIỄN ═══════════
• Tựa: Hàm Tử – Chương Dương 1285 – Trần Quang Khải  |  EN: The Battle of Hàm Tử – Chương Dương (1285)
• Khung hình 16:9 (bản 9:16 dựng lại bố cục dọc, không cắt xén) · Thời lượng ≈ 64 giây theo giọng đọc
• Giọng: lời dẫn Manus (nam trầm, ấm, kể chậm) là BẢN THU NGOÀI – dựng hình theo giọng, không cho mô hình video nói
• Era pack: 13th-century Đại Việt, Trần dynasty (spring–summer 1285) · Bối cảnh: the Red River near Hàm Tử landing and the Chương Dương fort south of Thăng Long, northern Vietnam, reed banks and earthen ramparts · Màu: vermilion red, bronze gold, earth brown, indigo, moss green, river grey · Nhạc: thunderous war drums, buffalo horns, bamboo flute, bronze gongs
• Sự thật & minh họa: lời dẫn bám nguồn (Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, SGK Lịch sử 7, Báo Nhân Dân, Znews, Wikipedia – Trận Chương Dương); cảnh người que là minh họa, không bịa thêm câu nói hay số liệu.
• Cast card (lặp ĐẦY ĐỦ trong mọi prompt):
  - COMMANDER (Trần Quang Khải): a Vietnamese Trần-dynasty commander in his fifties: short black beard with a little grey, firm straight eyebrows, a red cloth headband, red-and-gold lacquered lamellar armor over a deep crimson robe, a short red cape, black boots, a straight sword at the hip. Same white round head and short white neck as the drawing anchor, thin black limbs, compact black mitten palms.
  - ĐẠI VIỆT SOLDIERS (uniform group): Trần dynasty Vietnamese soldiers with "Sát Thát" ink tattoos on their arms, brown and indigo tunics, leather lamellar vests, conical hats, war boats with red banners; commanders in red-and-gold lacquered armor. Drawn with the same white heads, short white necks, thin black limbs and black mitten palms; no readable tattoo letters (only abstract ink marks).
  - ENEMY SOLDIERS – Nguyên Mông (uniform group): Yuan-Mongol troops in iron-studded leather armor and fur-trimmed helmets aboard Yuan war junks with blue banners, a fortified riverside camp. Same drawing grammar; cartoon depiction, not a caricature of any ethnicity.

Lời dẫn Manus (gốc giờ dựng, KHÔNG đưa vào prompt): "Năm 1285, trên dòng sông Hồng, quân dân nhà Trần liên tiếp đánh tan quân Nguyên ở Hàm Tử và Chương Dương, mở màn cuộc tổng phản công giải phóng kinh thành."

▶ CLIP 1
stickman animated video, 16:9, 8 seconds, 24 fps.

CHARACTER: A 2D cartoon man with a round white head and a short, sturdy white neck growing naturally from his clothed shoulders and supporting his jaw. The white head and neck form one continuous filled silhouette. The lower jaw flows directly into the short white neck; the bottom of that neck enters the tunic neckline. The visible connection is white, with black outlines only along its two sides. Keep this white connection visible during walking, turns, tilts and camera cuts. Use balanced adult stickman proportions rather than a big-headed chibi: the complete standing figure is roughly four head-heights tall, the round head is visibly narrower than the clothed shoulder span, the torso is slightly longer than the head, and the thin black legs provide a natural lower-body length. The exposed neck is short, about one tenth of the head diameter tall. Thin black arms and legs, exactly two of each. Polished 2D cel-shaded historical cartoon, clean thick black outlines, softly shaded period clothing, and sunlit painterly surroundings.
FACE: An appealing, expressive storybook-cartoon face on the round white head. Two solid black vertical oval eyes; character-specific eyebrows, nose and mouth defined in the cast card, not a universal template face. Subtle cheek shading gives the face warmth without realistic skin texture. Expressions react to the action while the eye shape, nose shape, face proportions and identity remain unchanged. Hair and facial hair, when present in the cast, have a few clearly shaped locks and soft cel shading, not flat featureless patches. Keep this readable cartoon richness, not a blank symbolic face, photorealism or detailed irises.
PALMS: Each thin black wrist ends in one compact, solid black rounded oval palm with a subtle integrated thumb bump. Use the same palm-to-head proportion in both hands and all shots: total palm silhouette about 18% of head diameter long and 10% wide, including the thumb bump. It is visibly wider than the wrist, neither a wrist-tip dot nor an enlarged glove. No separate fingers, finger lines or white fill. The empty indicating gesture keeps this compact silhouette; change the arm angle instead of spreading or enlarging the palm.
EXPOSED LIMBS: Every uncovered arm, forearm, wrist and shin is one slender solid black stroke. Face and short neck use white fill; the limbs use solid black strokes. Preserve the character proportions and costume colors throughout.

STYLE: polished 2D cel-shaded historical cartoon, full-color painted backgrounds. ERA: 13th-century Đại Việt, Trần dynasty (spring–summer 1285). SETTING: the Red River near Hàm Tử landing and the Chương Dương fort south of Thăng Long, northern Vietnam, reed banks and earthen ramparts; humid early summer morning with river haze. PALETTE: vermilion red, bronze gold, earth brown, indigo, moss green, river grey.

CAST: COMMANDER (Trần Quang Khải): a Vietnamese Trần-dynasty commander in his fifties: short black beard with a little grey, firm straight eyebrows, a red cloth headband, red-and-gold lacquered lamellar armor over a deep crimson robe, a short red cape, black boots, a straight sword at the hip. Same white round head and short white neck as the drawing anchor, thin black limbs, compact black mitten palms. Drawn with the same white heads, short white necks, thin black limbs and black mitten palms; no readable tattoo letters (only abstract ink marks). ENEMY SOLDIERS – Nguyên Mông (uniform group): Yuan-Mongol troops in iron-studded leather armor and fur-trimmed helmets aboard Yuan war junks with blue banners, a fortified riverside camp. Same drawing grammar; cartoon depiction, not a caricature of any ethnicity.

OPENING: establish the location and screen direction (Đại Việt on the left moving right, enemy on the right).

ACTION: [0–3s] wide lateral tracking shot: a column of soldiers marches left to right along the riverbank, spears upright, the flag bearer at the front. [3–6s] medium shot: feet stamp in rhythm, dust rises, the commander rides alongside and raises a hand. [6–8s] the column crests a low hill and the camera rises to reveal the landscape ahead.

SOUND: no speech from characters; external narration is added in editing. Era score: thunderous war drums, buffalo horns, bamboo flute, bronze gongs. Timed SFX that match the action.
