Dán từng PROMPT CLIP vào Veo / Google Flow (chế độ text-to-video, 16:9, 8 giây). Nên tạo trước ảnh tham chiếu nhân vật (chính diện / nghiêng / sau lưng) và đính kèm mỗi lần tạo để giữ nhân vật nhất quán.

═══════════ PHASE A – ĐỀ XUẤT ĐẠO DIỄN ═══════════
• Tựa: Bạch Đằng 938 – Ngô Quyền  |  EN: The Battle of Bạch Đằng (938)
• Khung hình 16:9 (bản 9:16 dựng lại bố cục dọc, không cắt xén) · Thời lượng ≈ 107 giây theo giọng đọc
• Giọng: lời dẫn Manus (nam trầm, ấm, kể chậm) là BẢN THU NGOÀI – dựng hình theo giọng, không cho mô hình video nói
• Era pack: 10th-century Vietnam (year 938), Ngô Quyền era · Bối cảnh: the tidal Bạch Đằng estuary in northern Vietnam with limestone karst islands, mangrove banks and misty brown water · Màu: deep river blue, earth brown, vermilion red, bamboo green, misty grey · Nhạc: deep war drums, low bamboo flute, plucked zither
• Sự thật & minh họa: lời dẫn bám nguồn (Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, Wikipedia – Trận Bạch Đằng (938), Báo Nhân Dân, Báo Giáo dục Việt Nam); cảnh người que là minh họa, không bịa thêm câu nói hay số liệu.
• Cast card (lặp ĐẦY ĐỦ trong mọi prompt):
  - COMMANDER (Ngô Quyền): a Vietnamese general of the 10th century in his forties: short black beard and moustache drawn as a few cel-shaded locks, determined thick eyebrows, a dark red cloth headscarf tied at the back, a brown lamellar leather cuirass over a long indigo robe, a red sash, black boots, a straight bronze sword at the hip. Same white round head and short white neck as the drawing anchor, thin black limbs, compact black mitten palms.
  - ĐẠI VIỆT SOLDIERS (uniform group): Vietnamese soldiers of Ngô Quyền in plain brown hemp tunics and loincloths, conical palm-leaf hats, round rattan shields, bronze-tipped spears and bows. Drawn with the same white heads, short white necks, thin black limbs and black mitten palms; no readable tattoo letters (only abstract ink marks).
  - ENEMY SOLDIERS – Nam Hán (uniform group): Southern Han (Nam Hán) Chinese marines in red-lacquered leather armor and iron helmets aboard tall multi-deck war junks. Same drawing grammar; cartoon depiction, not a caricature of any ethnicity.

Lời dẫn Manus (gốc giờ dựng, KHÔNG đưa vào prompt): "Năm 938, trên dòng sông Bạch Đằng, quân dân ta dưới sự chỉ huy của Ngô Quyền đã đánh tan đạo thủy quân Nam Hán, mở ra kỷ nguyên độc lập lâu dài cho dân tộc."

▶ CLIP 1 (phần 1/2)
stickman animated video, 16:9, 8 seconds, 24 fps.

CHARACTER: A 2D cartoon man with a round white head and a short, sturdy white neck growing naturally from his clothed shoulders and supporting his jaw. The white head and neck form one continuous filled silhouette. The lower jaw flows directly into the short white neck; the bottom of that neck enters the tunic neckline. The visible connection is white, with black outlines only along its two sides. Keep this white connection visible during walking, turns, tilts and camera cuts. Use balanced adult stickman proportions rather than a big-headed chibi: the complete standing figure is roughly four head-heights tall, the round head is visibly narrower than the clothed shoulder span, the torso is slightly longer than the head, and the thin black legs provide a natural lower-body length. The exposed neck is short, about one tenth of the head diameter tall. Thin black arms and legs, exactly two of each. Polished 2D cel-shaded historical cartoon, clean thick black outlines, softly shaded period clothing, and sunlit painterly surroundings.
FACE: An appealing, expressive storybook-cartoon face on the round white head. Two solid black vertical oval eyes; character-specific eyebrows, nose and mouth defined in the cast card, not a universal template face. Subtle cheek shading gives the face warmth without realistic skin texture. Expressions react to the action while the eye shape, nose shape, face proportions and identity remain unchanged. Hair and facial hair, when present in the cast, have a few clearly shaped locks and soft cel shading, not flat featureless patches. Keep this readable cartoon richness, not a blank symbolic face, photorealism or detailed irises.
PALMS: Each thin black wrist ends in one compact, solid black rounded oval palm with a subtle integrated thumb bump. Use the same palm-to-head proportion in both hands and all shots: total palm silhouette about 18% of head diameter long and 10% wide, including the thumb bump. It is visibly wider than the wrist, neither a wrist-tip dot nor an enlarged glove. No separate fingers, finger lines or white fill. The empty indicating gesture keeps this compact silhouette; change the arm angle instead of spreading or enlarging the palm.
EXPOSED LIMBS: Every uncovered arm, forearm, wrist and shin is one slender solid black stroke. Face and short neck use white fill; the limbs use solid black strokes. Preserve the character proportions and costume colors throughout.

STYLE: polished 2D cel-shaded historical cartoon, full-color painted backgrounds. ERA: 10th-century Vietnam (year 938), Ngô Quyền era. SETTING: the tidal Bạch Đằng estuary in northern Vietnam with limestone karst islands, mangrove banks and misty brown water; winter dusk, cold grey light. PALETTE: deep river blue, earth brown, vermilion red, bamboo green, misty grey.

CAST: COMMANDER (Ngô Quyền): a Vietnamese general of the 10th century in his forties: short black beard and moustache drawn as a few cel-shaded locks, determined thick eyebrows, a dark red cloth headscarf tied at the back, a brown lamellar leather cuirass over a long indigo robe, a red sash, black boots, a straight bronze sword at the hip. Same white round head and short white neck as the drawing anchor, thin black limbs, compact black mitten palms. ĐẠI VIỆT SOLDIERS (uniform group): Vietnamese soldiers of Ngô Quyền in plain brown hemp tunics and loincloths, conical palm-leaf hats, round rattan shields, bronze-tipped spears and bows. Drawn with the same white heads, short white necks, thin black limbs and black mitten palms; no readable tattoo letters (only abstract ink marks). ENEMY SOLDIERS – Nam Hán (uniform group): Southern Han (Nam Hán) Chinese marines in red-lacquered leather armor and iron helmets aboard tall multi-deck war junks. Same drawing grammar; cartoon depiction, not a caricature of any ethnicity.

OPENING: establish the location and screen direction (Đại Việt on the left moving right, enemy on the right).

ACTION: [0–3s] wide lateral tracking shot: a column of soldiers marches left to right along the riverbank, spears upright, the flag bearer at the front. [3–6s] medium shot: feet stamp in rhythm, dust rises, the commander rides alongside and raises a hand. [6–8s] the column crests a low hill and the camera rises to reveal the landscape ahead.

SOUND: no speech from characters; external narration is added in editing. Era score: deep war drums, low bamboo flute, plucked zither. Timed SFX that match the action.
