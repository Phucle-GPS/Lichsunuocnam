Phong cách A5 Historical Cartoon – quy trình skill directing-stickman-videos (kaomei/stickman-video-director, MIT).
Dán từng PROMPT CLIP vào Veo / Google Flow (chế độ text-to-video, 16:9, 8 giây). Nên tạo trước ảnh tham chiếu nhân vật (chính diện / nghiêng / sau lưng) và đính kèm mỗi lần tạo để giữ nhân vật nhất quán.

═══════════ PHASE A – ĐỀ XUẤT ĐẠO DIỄN ═══════════
• Tựa: Như Nguyệt 1077 – Lý Thường Kiệt  |  EN: The Battle of Như Nguyệt (1077)
• Khung hình 16:9 (bản 9:16 dựng lại bố cục dọc, không cắt xén) · Thời lượng ≈ 107 giây theo giọng đọc
• Giọng: lời dẫn Manus (nam trầm, ấm, kể chậm) là BẢN THU NGOÀI – dựng hình theo giọng, không cho mô hình video nói
• Era pack: 11th-century Đại Việt, Lý dynasty (year 1077) · Bối cảnh: the Như Nguyệt (Cầu) river in northern Vietnam with a long bamboo-stake palisade and earthen ramparts on the southern bank, rice fields and low hills · Màu: crimson, bronze gold, forest green, river grey, warm tan · Nhạc: war drums, horns, đàn tranh zither
• Sự thật & minh họa: lời dẫn bám nguồn (Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, SGK Lịch sử 7, Wikipedia – Trận Như Nguyệt, Tạp chí Quản lý Nhà nước); cảnh người que là minh họa, không bịa thêm câu nói hay số liệu.
• Cast card (lặp ĐẦY ĐỦ trong mọi prompt):
  - COMMANDER (Lý Thường Kiệt): a Vietnamese Lý-dynasty grand general in his late fifties: neat grey beard, calm wise eyebrows, a black gauze official hat with two short side wings, red-and-gold lacquered lamellar armor over a crimson robe, a red cape, black boots, a long sword at the hip. Same white round head and short white neck as the drawing anchor, thin black limbs, compact black mitten palms.
  - ĐẠI VIỆT SOLDIERS (uniform group): Lý dynasty Vietnamese soldiers in indigo cotton tunics with leather lamellar vests, conical hats, long spears and crossbows, the characters "Thiên tử binh" inked on their foreheads. Drawn with the same white heads, short white necks, thin black limbs and black mitten palms; no readable tattoo letters (only abstract ink marks).
  - ENEMY SOLDIERS – Tống (uniform group): Song dynasty Chinese infantry in heavy iron lamellar armor with red tassels and crossbowmen, siege rafts. Same drawing grammar; cartoon depiction, not a caricature of any ethnicity.

━━━━ CẢNH 1 (title) · 0.0–11.1s · hành động: hanhquan ━━━━
Lời dẫn Manus (gốc giờ dựng, KHÔNG đưa vào prompt): "Năm 1077, bên bờ sông Như Nguyệt, quân dân Đại Việt thời Lý đã chặn đứng mười vạn quân Tống, buộc kẻ thù phải rút về nước."

▶ CLIP 1 (phần 1/2)
stickman animated video, 16:9, 8 seconds, 24 fps.

CHARACTER: A 2D cartoon man with a round white head and a short, sturdy white neck growing naturally from his clothed shoulders and supporting his jaw. The white head and neck form one continuous filled silhouette. The lower jaw flows directly into the short white neck; the bottom of that neck enters the tunic neckline. The visible connection is white, with black outlines only along its two sides. Keep this white connection visible during walking, turns, tilts and camera cuts. Use balanced adult stickman proportions rather than a big-headed chibi: the complete standing figure is roughly four head-heights tall, the round head is visibly narrower than the clothed shoulder span, the torso is slightly longer than the head, and the thin black legs provide a natural lower-body length. The exposed neck is short, about one tenth of the head diameter tall. Thin black arms and legs, exactly two of each. Polished 2D cel-shaded historical cartoon, clean thick black outlines, softly shaded period clothing, and sunlit painterly surroundings.
FACE: An appealing, expressive storybook-cartoon face on the round white head. Two solid black vertical oval eyes; character-specific eyebrows, nose and mouth defined in the cast card, not a universal template face. Subtle cheek shading gives the face warmth without realistic skin texture. Expressions react to the action while the eye shape, nose shape, face proportions and identity remain unchanged. Hair and facial hair, when present in the cast, have a few clearly shaped locks and soft cel shading, not flat featureless patches. Keep this readable cartoon richness, not a blank symbolic face, photorealism or detailed irises.
PALMS: Each thin black wrist ends in one compact, solid black rounded oval palm with a subtle integrated thumb bump. Use the same palm-to-head proportion in both hands and all shots: total palm silhouette about 18% of head diameter long and 10% wide, including the thumb bump. It is visibly wider than the wrist, neither a wrist-tip dot nor an enlarged glove. No separate fingers, finger lines or white fill. The empty indicating gesture keeps this compact silhouette; change the arm angle instead of spreading or enlarging the palm.
EXPOSED LIMBS: Every uncovered arm, forearm, wrist and shin is one slender solid black stroke. Face and short neck use white fill; the limbs use solid black strokes. Preserve the character proportions and costume colors throughout.

STYLE: polished 2D cel-shaded historical cartoon, full-color painted backgrounds. ERA: 11th-century Đại Việt, Lý dynasty (year 1077). SETTING: the Như Nguyệt (Cầu) river in northern Vietnam with a long bamboo-stake palisade and earthen ramparts on the southern bank, rice fields and low hills; early spring, drizzle and fog. PALETTE: crimson, bronze gold, forest green, river grey, warm tan.

CAST: COMMANDER (Lý Thường Kiệt): a Vietnamese Lý-dynasty grand general in his late fifties: neat grey beard, calm wise eyebrows, a black gauze official hat with two short side wings, red-and-gold lacquered lamellar armor over a crimson robe, a red cape, black boots, a long sword at the hip. Same white round head and short white neck as the drawing anchor, thin black limbs, compact black mitten palms. ĐẠI VIỆT SOLDIERS (uniform group): Lý dynasty Vietnamese soldiers in indigo cotton tunics with leather lamellar vests, conical hats, long spears and crossbows, the characters "Thiên tử binh" inked on their foreheads. Drawn with the same white heads, short white necks, thin black limbs and black mitten palms; no readable tattoo letters (only abstract ink marks). ENEMY SOLDIERS – Tống (uniform group): Song dynasty Chinese infantry in heavy iron lamellar armor with red tassels and crossbowmen, siege rafts. Same drawing grammar; cartoon depiction, not a caricature of any ethnicity.

OPENING: establish the location and screen direction (Đại Việt on the left moving right, enemy on the right).

ACTION: [0–3s] wide lateral tracking shot: a column of soldiers marches left to right along the riverbank, spears upright, the flag bearer at the front. [3–6s] medium shot: feet stamp in rhythm, dust rises, the commander rides alongside and raises a hand. [6–8s] the column crests a low hill and the camera rises to reveal the landscape ahead.

SOUND: no speech from characters; external narration is added in editing. Era score: war drums, horns, đàn tranh zither. Timed SFX that match the action.

NO VISIBLE TEXT, LETTERS, NUMBERS, SUBTITLES, LOGOS OR WATERMARKS (blank paper or abstract brush strokes only), NO SPEECH BUBBLES, NO BLOOD OR GORE, NO PHOTOREALISTIC FACES, NO 3D CGI, NO PAPER TEXTURE, NO EXTRA LIMBS, NO FUSED OR DUPLICATE HEADS, NO COSTUME CHANGES, NO EXTRA CHARACTERS, NO INDIVIDUAL FINGERS, NO FINGER SEPARATIONS, NO WHITE HANDS OR GLOVES, NO FLOATING HEAD, NO BACKGROUND GAP BETWEEN HEAD AND NECKLINE.
